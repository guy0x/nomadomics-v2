# Wise vs Revolut vs Payoneer: The Honest 2026 Comparison

Picture this: you're three coffees deep in a Lisbon café, about to invoice a client in dollars, pay rent in euros, and grab dinner in a currency you still can't pronounce. One account moves all three. The other two quietly skim a few bucks off every hop. The difference between picking right and picking wrong? Easily $400–900 a year for someone living across borders.

Let's be real — most "comparisons" are affiliate bait that tells you all three are great. They're not. They're built for different people, and if you pick the wrong one you're the one funding their "free" tier. Here's the actual math.

## The one number that matters: FX spread

Forget the headline "no fees." Every one of these三家 makes money on the spread — the gap between the real mid-market rate and what they give you. That's where your money leaks.

- **Wise** — uses the real mid-market rate, then charges a transparent 0.4–1.0% fee on top (varies by currency). For EUR→USD that's about 0.4%. Honest, predictable, no games.
- **Revolut** — on the free plan, weekends carry a 1.0% markup (markets are closed, they hedge = you pay). Weekdays are near mid-market up to your monthly exchange limit (~£1,000 on Standard), then ~0.5% above. The catch: most nomads move money on weekends. Do the math on your own flow.
- **Payoneer** — this is where it gets ugly. Receiving from another Payoneer user is "free," but currency conversion runs ~2–3.5% above mid-market, and incoming wire-style payments (like from Upwork or a US client via ACH) hit 0–1% receiving + the spread. For a freelancer pulling $4,000/month, that spread alone can cost $80–140/mo.

**Bottom line on spreads:** Wise wins on transparency, Revolut wins if you never touch money on weekends, Payoneer is the most expensive by a mile unless someone's paying you *into* Payoneer directly.

## ATM withdrawals — the travel tax

You will need cash. Here's the ceiling before fees kick in:

- **Wise** — free up to ~$100–350/month depending on region (e.g. $350 in the US, less in some markets), then ~2% + local ATM operator fees. Reasonable.
- **Revolut** — free up to £200/month on Standard, then 2% (min £1). Tight if you're in a cash-heavy country (hello, Germany, Japan).
- **Payoneer** — doesn't really do a consumer debit card with generous ATM terms; their card is more for receiving/ spending received funds, and ATM fees run $3–5 + spread per withdrawal. Not your travel card.

If you're pulling cash weekly in a cheap country, Wise's higher free tier saves you real money over a year.

## Who each one is actually for

### Wise — the default for most nomads
You invoice in one currency, live in another, and want to *know* your cost. The multi-currency account holds 40+ currencies, you get local bank details (EUR IBAN, GBP sort code, US routing) so clients pay you like a local. Best for: freelancers, consultants, anyone paid in a different currency than they spend.

### Revolut — the daily spender
Slick app, great for day-to-day card spending, crypto on the side, and instant peer transfers. Best if you mostly *spend* abroad rather than *receive* large invoices, and you can keep conversions to weekdays. The Standard plan is free; Metal (~$15–20/mo) drops the weekend markup and bumps ATM limits — worth it if you move serious volume.

### Payoneer — the marketplace earner
If you're on Upwork, Fiverr, or selling on Amazon/eBay, Payoneer is often the *only* payout option, and receiving from another Payoneer user is free. Best when the platform forces it. Worst as your primary account because of the spread. Open it for the payouts, move money to Wise fast.

## The trap nobody mentions

"Free account" doesn't mean free money movement. Three specific leaks:

1. **Idle balance decay** — none of these pay meaningful interest on defaults (Revolut Vaults/Savings exist in some regions; Wise has interest in some currencies). Your sitting balance is roughly static while inflation eats it. Park idle cash in a high-yield account, not here.
2. **Plan creep** — Revolut and Payoneer both nudge you toward paid tiers. Only upgrade if the math pays for itself (Revolut Metal's weekend waiver + ATM bump clears $15–20/mo only at volume).
3. **The Payoneer receiving fee on cards** — getting paid via the Payoneer card or certain "mass payout" channels can stack a receiving fee *and* the spread. Read the specific payment method, not the marketing.

## Our honest pick

- **Marketplace sellers:** Payoneer for payouts only, sweep to Wise weekly.
- **High-volume movers:** Revolut Metal + Wise.

Open Wise first. It's the one account that won't surprise you with a spread you didn't model.
