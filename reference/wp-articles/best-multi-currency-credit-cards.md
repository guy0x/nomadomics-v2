Traveling internationally has never been easier, but if you use the wrong credit card, hidden fees can take a big bite out of your budget. Foreign transaction charges, bad exchange rates, and ATM withdrawal costs can quietly add up to hundreds of dollars on a single trip. 



That’s why choosing the right multi-currency credit card is essential. These cards eliminate unnecessary fees, give you access to better exchange rates, and often reward you with points, miles, or cashback. In 2025, they’ve become an indispensable tool for digital nomads, frequent flyers, and anyone who wants to stretch their travel budget further.



Top 10 Multi-Currency Credit Cards



A multi-currency credit card isn’t just about convenience — it’s about protecting your money while making the most of your spending abroad. Traditional credit cards often tack on hidden fees, weak exchange rates, and other charges that slowly erode your budget. 



Multi-currency cards solve this problem by eliminating foreign transaction fees, offering fair conversion rates, and rewarding you for purchases made internationally. They often go beyond savings by adding travel-friendly perks like insurance and lounge access, which can transform the overall travel experience.



Chase Sapphire Preferred




No foreign transaction fees.



2x–5x rewards on travel and dining.



Transfer points to airline and hotel partners.



Strong built-in travel protections.



Annual fee: $95.




The Chase Sapphire Preferred remains one of the most versatile travel cards on the market. Its combination of strong earning rates on dining and travel, along with flexible redemption options through transfer partners, makes it highly valuable for frequent travelers. Add in its comprehensive travel protections and the lack of foreign transaction fees, and it’s easy to see why this card is often the top recommendation for those who want premium benefits without an excessive annual fee.



Capital One Venture Rewards




No foreign transaction fees.



Flat 2x miles on every purchase.



Easy redemption as credits or transfers.



Annual fee: $95.




The Capital One Venture Rewards card is ideal for travelers who want simplicity. Instead of juggling multiple categories, you earn the same strong rewards rate on every purchase. Redemption is straightforward — you can erase travel purchases from your statement or transfer miles to partners. The lack of foreign transaction fees makes it reliable abroad, while the reasonable annual fee ensures the card pays for itself quickly with even moderate use.



American Express Gold




4x points on dining worldwide.



Generous grocery rewards.



Premium lifestyle perks.



Annual fee: $250.




The American Express Gold card is particularly appealing for travelers who prioritize food and lifestyle perks. Earning four points per dollar on dining worldwide makes it a standout choice for those who spend heavily in restaurants at home and abroad. 



Its grocery rewards add value for everyday spending, while premium Amex benefits enhance the travel experience. The higher annual fee is justified for frequent diners, but acceptance is limited depending on where you’re traveling.



Revolut Metal




Multi-currency wallet with real exchange rates.



Travel insurance and lounge access.



Cashback on purchases.



Monthly subscription fee.




Revolut Metal is designed for the digital-first traveler. It combines a premium card with a powerful multi-currency wallet, allowing you to hold and spend dozens of currencies at fair exchange rates. Perks like travel insurance, lounge access, and cashback make it a well-rounded option for nomads and frequent flyers who prefer managing everything through a sleek mobile app. Instead of a traditional annual fee, Revolut uses a subscription model, which may be easier for some travelers to budget.



Wise Multi-Currency Card




Linked to Wise account with 50+ currencies.



Real mid-market exchange rates.



Low-cost ATM withdrawals abroad.



No annual fee.




The Wise Multi-Currency Card is a top choice for budget-conscious travelers. It is linked directly to a Wise account, where you can hold and convert more than 50 currencies at the true mid-market rate. This means no hidden markups and full transparency on fees. It’s particularly valuable for those who frequently withdraw cash abroad, since Wise offers some of the lowest ATM costs available. While it doesn’t include premium perks like rewards or lounge access, its affordability and fairness make it a must-have for nomads.



HSBC Premier World Elite




Premium insurance and lounge access.



Concierge services worldwide.



Strong global acceptance.



High annual fee.




The HSBC Premier World Elite is tailored to high-net-worth travelers and global banking clients. It offers premium perks such as extensive travel insurance, lounge access, and concierge support. The card integrates seamlessly with HSBC’s global banking services, which is helpful for those managing finances across borders. While the high annual fee limits its appeal to everyday travelers, it’s an excellent option for frequent international flyers who want convenience and top-tier perks.



Barclaycard Arrival Plus




2x miles on every purchase.



Redeem miles as travel statement credits.



No foreign transaction fees.



Annual fee often waived first year.




The Barclaycard Arrival Plus provides consistent rewards across all spending categories, making it simple to use both at home and abroad. With two miles earned per dollar and the ability to redeem them directly as travel statement credits, this card offers excellent flexibility. The lack of foreign transaction fees ensures it performs well overseas, while the waived annual fee in the first year makes it an attractive starter card for frequent travelers.



Citi Premier Card




3x points on travel, dining, and groceries.



Multiple airline transfer partners.



No foreign transaction fees.



Annual fee: $95.




The Citi Premier Card is another strong mid-tier option for those who want both rewards and flexibility. It earns triple points in useful categories like travel, dining, and groceries, making it valuable whether you’re at home or abroad. Points can be transferred to multiple airline partners, expanding redemption opportunities. With no foreign transaction fees and a modest annual fee, it is a direct competitor to Chase Sapphire Preferred.



N26 You




Transparent exchange rates.



Mobile-first banking experience.



Travel insurance included.



Low monthly fee.




N26 You is a European challenger bank card designed for modern travelers. It emphasizes transparency, offering fair exchange rates and straightforward mobile management. Travel insurance adds a layer of security, while the low monthly fee makes it accessible. Although it lacks the global reach of premium U.S. cards, it is a strong option for EU-based nomads and long-term travelers who want a streamlined digital banking experience.



Revolut Standard




Free entry-level option.



Real exchange rates up to monthly cap.



Useful for budget-conscious travelers.



Limited perks compared to premium tiers.




Revolut Standard provides an easy entry point into the multi-currency world. With no annual fee, it gives travelers access to real exchange rates up to a set monthly limit. While it lacks the perks of Revolut Metal and other premium cards, it is an excellent choice for budget-conscious travelers or those who need only occasional international spending support. Its digital-first platform ensures convenience and control while keeping costs low.



What’s the best travel credit card?



Multi-currency credit cards are no longer a luxury—they’re a necessity for anyone spending time abroad. They cut out hidden fees, provide access to real exchange rates, and unlock rewards that make travel more affordable.



For most travelers, the Chase Sapphire Preferred and Capital One Venture Rewards strike the best balance of affordability, rewards, and worldwide acceptance. Digital nomads will find excellent value in Wise and Revolut, while premium clients may prefer HSBC Premier for its exclusive perks.



The right card doesn’t just save you money —  it changes how you travel. With one in your wallet, you can focus on the journey instead of the fees.
